What to Wear

Come as you are, dressed for a garden

Our theme

Whimsical Garden Party

Honestly? Wear whatever you want — we just want you there. But if you’d like to play along, think florals, soft colors, and anything that feels like a spring afternoon in a garden. Have fun with it!

A Little Inspiration

For Her

  • Floral midi or maxi dresses in soft, flowy fabrics
  • Pastels and garden tones — pinks, lavenders, butter yellow, sage
  • Playful details like puff sleeves, ruffles, or tiered skirts
  • A dressy jumpsuit or pretty two-piece set
  • Block heels, wedges, or dressy flats for garden paths
  • Fun extras: pearls, floral hair clips, a statement earring

For Him

  • Suits in light gray, tan, sage, or navy
  • Lightweight fabrics like linen or cotton blends
  • Pastel or subtly patterned dress shirts
  • A floral tie, bow tie, or pocket square — or skip the tie altogether
  • Loafers or dress shoes in brown or tan
  • Bonus points for a flower in the lapel

A Few Practical Notes

March evenings in Atlanta can be cool, so bring a light layer — a wrap, shawl, or jacket will keep you comfortable as the sun goes down.

Comfortable shoes are your friend. There will be dancing!
