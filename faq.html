FAQ

Everything you need to know

What should I wear?

We’re going for garden party cocktail attire! Think romantic florals, playful colors, flowy dresses, and suits with a little personality. If you want to play along, our colors are eucalyptus, mint, coral, peach, sky blue, periwinkle, lavender, and butter yellow. Most importantly, wear something that makes you feel comfortable and confident!

Weather permitting, part of the celebration will be outdoors in a grassy area, and we’ll be dancing the night away, so keep that in mind when choosing your shoes. March evenings in Atlanta can get chilly, so we recommend bringing a light jacket or wrap.

The theme is just for fun, so regular cocktail attire is absolutely welcome. For more outfit ideas, open “What to Wear” under the wedding on our Schedule.

What about gifts?

Your presence at our wedding is truly the greatest gift we could ask for! We’re so grateful to celebrate this special day with our favorite people.

If you’d like to give us something extra as we start this next chapter together, we’ve put together a few things on our registry page.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but we’ve decided to keep our wedding ceremony and reception adults-only. We hope this gives everyone a chance to relax, celebrate, and enjoy a fun night out!

That said, children are absolutely welcome at our welcome drinks & desserts on Thursday and our brunch on Saturday! We’d love for them to be part of the weekend festivities. Please include them in your RSVP for those events so we can plan accordingly.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes! Complimentary parking is available on-site at Callanwolde Fine Arts Center.

That said, we encourage guests to use Uber or Lyft if they plan to enjoy a few drinks. We want everyone to celebrate freely and get home safely!

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by February 1, 2027, so we can finalize our guest count, seating arrangements, and all the little details that will make the weekend special.

Even if you can’t make it, please RSVP to let us know. We’ll miss you, but we completely understand!

I have a dietary restriction. Who do I tell?

We want everyone to have something delicious to eat! Please let us know about any dietary restrictions when you RSVP.
