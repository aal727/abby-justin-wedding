Getting Here

Atlanta is served by Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL), one of the busiest airports in the world with direct flights from most major cities. The Callanwolde Fine Arts Center is approximately 20 minutes from the airport.

Getting Around

Atlanta is a car-friendly city, but traffic can get pretty bad, especially during rush hour, so give yourself some extra time to get around. We recommend using rideshare apps like Uber or Lyft for easy travel between events. Parking is available at the venue.
